Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

AI Booklet Designs (aleksvilly.github.io/ai-booklet-designs) is a free service for generating editorial booklets. This page explains what data we collect and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Analytics data — we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site (pages visited, session duration, country, device type). This data is anonymised and aggregated. No personal identity is stored.
  • Generation requests — when you submit a booklet request, it is sent as a plain-text message to a public ntfy.sh channel. Do not include personal information (name, email, phone) in the request form.
  • Contact messages — if you use the contact form, your name, email or phone, and message are sent to us via Formspree. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry.
  • Local storage — your generation request history is stored only in your browser's local storage. We cannot access it.

2. Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish users. By continuing to use this site you consent to the placement of analytics cookies. You can opt out using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser settings.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics 4 — Google Privacy Policy
  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions — Formspree Privacy Policy
  • ntfy.sh — routes public generation requests — ntfy Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website — GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Google Fonts — loads typefaces for the UI — Google Privacy Policy
  • Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia — provide licensed images embedded in generated booklets. We store image URLs and attribution metadata only. No personal data from those services is stored.

4. Generated booklets

Generated booklets are published publicly as part of the collection at this website. Each booklet contains attribution and licence information for the photos used. Booklet requests are processed via a public queue — do not submit confidential or personal information in the topic or description fields.

5. Data retention

  • Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default.
  • Contact form messages are kept only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry.
  • Generation requests are public on ntfy.sh for up to 12 hours after submission.

6. Your rights

If you are located in the EU or UK, you have rights under GDPR / UK GDPR including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, or if you have any questions about this policy, contact us via the contact form.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version.
